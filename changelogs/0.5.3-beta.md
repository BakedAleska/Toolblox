# Toolblox 0.5.3 beta
*November 23, 2026*

## New

### Close all clients
- **Settings → General → Roblox → Close all** closes every running Roblox client at once, after you confirm. Clients that don't close within 5 seconds are force-closed. Auto-rejoin and disconnect notifications ignore the accounts it closes.

### Ask destination on each join
- Turn on **Settings → Accounts → Ask destination on each join** to pick a game or player every time you join. Joining several accounts at once, from **Join selected** or a group, asks once, and every account goes to that destination. Going back cancels the join.

## New

### Bootstrapper widget
- A new **Bootstrapper** widget in the catalogue makes many Roblox clients at once lighter on your computer, for filling a server with accounts. Windows only.
- Turn it on with the gauge button at the start of the search bar on **Accounts**. While it's on, the button is highlighted, and every account you join uses it. Hover it to see **Join with Bootstrapper**.
- **Profiles:** pick how much each client you're not focused on may use. **Watch** keeps clients you still look at smooth, **Background** is for clients you rarely look at, and **AFK** uses the least. Clients are limited as soon as they open, and the one you're focused on always runs normally.
- Each profile sets a **CPU cap** (a hard share of the whole CPU), a **RAM cap** (memory over it moves to the page file), how many **CPU cores** a client may use, **CPU** and **GPU priority**, and **Efficiency mode**. Limited clients also get low memory priority, so Windows takes RAM from them first.
- **Custom:** change any limit, well past the profiles if you like. Very low caps can make a client stutter or disconnect while it loads. If Windows refuses a limit, the page shows which one under **Not applied**.
- **Frame rate cap:** off by default. It sets Roblox's own **Maximum frame rate** for every client opened while it's on, including the one you're playing in, from 5 to 240 FPS, and puts your own setting back when turned off. Roblox's menu offers 30, 60, 75, 120, 144, 165, and 240, and may ignore other values.
- Change these on the **Bootstrapper** widget page.
- **Server location:** each time a client joins or teleports to a server, a small message slides down at the top of the screen with the city the server is in, such as **Atlanta, Georgia, US**. It reads the server address from Roblox's own log and looks it up on ipinfo.io. Off by default. Turn it on under **Joins** on the **Bootstrapper** widget page. It works whether or not the Bootstrapper is on for joins.

## New

### Rogue Lineage widget
- Two new tabs on the Rogue Lineage widget page, **Snap training** and **Blacksmith**. Each has its own power button and a key that starts and stops it while Roblox is focused, **F3** and **F4** by default. A label at the top of the Roblox window shows the task while it runs, with its progress and the key that stops it. Both stop as soon as Roblox isn't focused.
- **Snap training:** choose the spell to train from its own page, which lists all 15 spells with a snap variant and has a search field. Put the spell on your hotbar and its key is found automatically, by reading the hotbar like the cooldown overlay does. Training casts at the spell's own range, not its snap range: **Snap Ignis** trains at Ignis's 85–95%. It holds **G** until your mana reaches the spell's range, then casts the spell and puts it away before the cast finishes, again and again, while tapping **G** to keep mana up. When mana drops below the range it stops casting and charges again. It casts only in the middle of the range, 60% by default; change this in **Settings → Rogue Lineage → Snap training**. The mana bar overlay doesn't need to be on.
- **Blacksmith:** add a step for each item, with its hotbar key and how many times to click the anvil. Select **Set** beside **Anvil** and **Hammer**, then click each one in Roblox. It picks each item before every anvil click, then clicks the hammer, for as many weapons as you set. It stops early when a step's hotbar slot is empty. The waits between clicks are in **Settings → Rogue Lineage → Blacksmith**.
- **Game sound:** while snap training is on, the widget listens to Roblox's sound, and only Roblox's, for the mana-full chime and spell casts. The chime ends charging right away, and the **Snap training** tile shows how many chimes and casts it heard. Nothing is recorded or stored. Turn it off in **Settings → Rogue Lineage → Snap training → Game sound**. It needs Windows 10 version 2004 or later.
- These are the only Rogue Lineage features that send keys and clicks.

## Changes

### Widget mark
- Anything a widget adds to Toolblox's own pages, such as the **Join with Bootstrapper** button on **Accounts**, now has the same small puzzle dot at its corner. Hover it to see which widget it came from.
- Options one widget offers in another widget's page, such as Rogue Lineage's **Pause when backpack is open** on the Autoclicker page, use the same dot.

### Autoclicker widget
- The Autoclicker's own window, opened with the pop-out button on its page, now stays above other windows, including Roblox.

### Joining several accounts
- Joining several accounts, from **Join selected** or a group, is now a queue: each account waits until the one before it is in game, so a slow computer no longer gets many clients loading at once. If an account isn't in game after 2 minutes, the next one starts anyway.
- The **⏱** join delay button is gone. A saved delay is no longer used.

### Destination picker
- The **Next join only** button now sits at the end of the search field.
- The **No default** button is gone from the destination picker. Clear the search and press **Enter** to remove the default destination and go back to the general note and tags. This also removes an account's or group's own destination when picking one for it.

### Group notes
- The **Note** field is gone from the group editor. Select below a group's account count on **Accounts** to write its note, the same way as an account's.

### Account actions
- The **Edit note** and **Open Roblox profile** buttons are gone from an account's **…** actions. Select the note to edit it.

### Friend requests and follows
- When Roblox blocks a friend request or follow, often with a captcha, the message now has an **Open on Roblox** button. It opens the player's Roblox profile in a window already signed in as that account, so you can solve the check and send the request there. The window keeps nothing after you close it.
- In player search, selecting a player's avatar opens their profile signed in as the account in the top right menu, instead of in your browser. Their friend and follow status updates when you return to Toolblox.

## Changes

### Rogue Lineage widget
- The cooldown overlay supports about 40 more skills, with cooldowns from the Rogue Lineage Assist Tool's class profiles, such as **Pommel Strike**, **Triple Slash**, **Calm Mind**, **Lightning Elbow**, and **Lethality**.
- The mana reading ignores a few odd rows across the bar, so a line or effect over it no longer cuts the percentage short.
- The mana bar is also found in the brighter interface some scenes show.
- Every tab now has a **How to use** line under its power button, showing the next step to set it up. Select it to see what the feature does and every step, each checked off as you do it. Snap training and Blacksmith show **Needs setup** and what's missing until they're ready to start.
- The tabs are now **Mana bar**, **Cooldowns**, **Snap training**, and **Blacksmith**, and **Tracked spells** is now **Spell ranges**.
- A fresh look for the Rogue Lineage page. The tabs sit together in one strip, with a line between the features that only read the screen and the ones that send keys and clicks. A dot beside each tab's name shows whether its feature is off, on, working, or needs something, and what it needs shows under the name, such as **Needs setup**.
- **Spell ranges** on the **Mana bar** tab now draws a mana bar like the one in game, with each spell's range in its color and a line at your current mana.
- **Snap training** draws the chosen spell's range on the same bar, with the part it casts in filled in.
- The Gate backfire warning is gone from the **Mana bar** tab, along with its **Warning sound** setting.

## Fixes

### Joining players
- Fixed **The player isn't in a game** when following a player whose privacy settings hide their game from the joining account. The join now goes ahead, and Roblox shows its own message if it can't follow them.

### Widgets
- Long widget descriptions on **Widgets** can now be read in full. Select a cut-off description to show all of it over the card, and select **×** or press **Esc** to close it.
- When the catalogue is empty or couldn't be loaded, **Widgets** no longer scrolls down to it.
- Widget updates now show up without restarting Toolblox. The catalogue is checked again when you return to Toolblox, at most every 10 minutes, and an **Update** button appears on any widget with a newer version.
- If the catalogue couldn't be loaded, such as when Toolblox starts before the internet connection is ready, it's tried again 30 seconds later when you return to Toolblox, instead of waiting for **Retry**.

### Rogue Lineage widget
- Fixed **The Rogue Lineage helper isn't responding** appearing while the widget was busy, such as with debug mode on or the backpack watched for the Autoclicker. The widget sent more requests than the app allows each second, and now stays within the limit.
- Fixed the Rogue Lineage overlays showing **Not supported** on some Windows 10 computers. On Windows 11 the widget now reads the Roblox window directly with Windows.Graphics.Capture, which is faster. On Windows 10 it falls back to this when the screen can't be read without the overlays. Windows 10 may show a yellow border around Roblox while an overlay is on.
- If no way of reading Roblox works, **Not supported** now says which Windows build it ran on and the error Windows returned. The Toolblox log also notes which way the widget reads Roblox.

### Multiple instances
- With **Allow multiple Roblox instances** on, Toolblox no longer changes Roblox windows that are already open when it opens another account. This targets the bug where closing one window, often the first one opened, closed every other account's window too. Close any Roblox windows opened before this update, then open them again.
