# Toolblox 0.5.1 beta
*Unreleased*

## Changes
- **Widgets** now opens at your installed widgets, then glides down to the catalogue. Select the **Catalogue** heading below your installed widgets to scroll there too. Scrolling or clicking stops the glide. With animations off, it opens at the catalogue. Turn on **Open widgets at installed** in **Settings → General → Interface** to stay at your installed widgets instead.
- Installed widgets on **Widgets** now sit in one row that scrolls sideways, instead of a grid.
