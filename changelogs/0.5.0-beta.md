# Toolblox 0.5.0 beta
*November 17, 2026*

## New

### Join a player
- A destination, the default or an account's own, can now be a player as well as a game. **Join** then follows that player into the game they're in.
- If the player isn't in a game, the join stops and says so.
- On the dashboard, select an online friend who's in a game to join them as the **Continue as** account.

### Account groups
- Group accounts to join them together or to sort them, such as by the games you play on them. Select the **New group** button (folder with a plus) at the top of **Accounts**, or select accounts and press the folder button in the selection bar.
- A group has a name, a note, and any accounts, set on its own page with **Select all** for the account list. It shows the first three accounts' pictures stacked, with a count of the rest.
- **Join** on a group joins every account in it, with the join delay between them. Press it again while it's loading to cancel.
- A group can have its own destination, a game or a player. Without one, each account joins its own destination.
- **Add to group** among an account's **⋯** buttons adds it to or removes it from any group, or starts a **New group** with it.
- The **⋯** buttons on a group edit it, select its accounts, set or clear its destination, and delete it. Deleting a group keeps its accounts.
- Groups are listed under **Groups**, below your accounts. The **★** on a group pins the group itself, not its accounts: it moves to **Pinned** on **Accounts** and shows on the dashboard.
- Search on **Accounts** also finds groups by name, note, or account.

### Changelog
- **Settings → About → Changelog** shows what changed in each version. Pick a version from the menu at the top right.
- The first time Toolblox opens after an update, it shows **What's new** for the new version. **Continue** goes to the dashboard. It doesn't show again until the next update.

### Destinations and player search
- On **Accounts**, the pin button beside the search field shows the default destination when you hover it, and opens destination search. After you pick one, the pin briefly shows what was saved.
- **Set destination** among an account's **⋯** buttons opens the same search for that account. **Use default destination** clears it.
- Select the icon at the start of the search bar to switch between **Games** and **Players**, keeping what you typed. Before you type, it lists your **Recent** games or players, with the current one marked. Remove one with its **✕**.
- Select a game or player to save it as the destination. Select a player's picture to open their Roblox profile.
- The menu at the top right chooses which account acts on the players you find. Each player's row has buttons to **Add friend**, accept or decline a friend request, **Unfriend**, **Follow** or **Unfollow**, and **Block** or **Unblock**. Hover a button to see what it does. Unfriending and blocking ask first.
- If Roblox asks for a verification check, such as a captcha, Toolblox says so. Do that action on roblox.com instead.

## Changes
- On **Accounts**, the search field fills the bar, and the default place field is replaced by the destination pin. Search matches each word you type in names or notes, in any order, and shows how many accounts match. In compact mode, a matching note shows under the account. **⌘F** (**Ctrl+F** on Windows) jumps to it, and **Esc** clears it.
- Pinned accounts now show at the top of **Accounts**, under their own **Pinned** heading.
- **Select accounts**, the checklist button at the top right of **Accounts**, starts selecting accounts to join together. It replaces **Select** in each account's menu. Double-clicking an account still starts a selection.
- **Join delay** moved from **Settings** to the selection bar on **Accounts**. Select the **⏱** button after **Join selected** to open a box, and type the seconds between accounts, from 0 to 60. Hover the button to see the current delay. Your saved delay carries over.
- Pinning or unpinning an account is now instant.
- **Show game names** is gone from **Settings**. On **Accounts**, an account's status badge reads **In game**, and hovering it shows the game, as on the dashboard.
- The **↻** button beside the destination search bar (**Next join only**) picks a one-time destination: the next join uses it, then the saved destination applies again. The pin and account rows show it with a ↻ icon, and it can be cleared there. A cancelled or failed join keeps it, and it's cleared when Toolblox restarts.
- Closing an account's **⋯** buttons now slides them shut.
- Press **Join** again while it's loading to cancel that join. With accounts selected, **Joining 3 of 5 · Cancel** in the selection bar cancels every selected account that hasn't opened Roblox yet. Accounts already in Roblox stay in game.
- The dashboard's **Online friends** now lists the friends of your pinned accounts, or of the last played account when none are pinned. Change it in **Settings → General → Dashboard → Online friends** to **All accounts**, **Pinned**, or **Last played**. Selecting a friend who's in a game joins them as the account they're friends with.

## More changes
- When **Accounts** is sorted by last played, each account shows when it was last played, such as **Last played 3h ago**. Hover it for the exact date and time.
- An account's star and **⋯** buttons now fade in when you hover the account, instead of sliding in.
- An account's **⋯** now opens into a row of buttons beside it instead of a menu: move up and down (in manual order), set or clear the destination, add to a group, edit the note, copy the username, open the Roblox profile, sign in again, and remove. Hover a button to see what it does. **⋯** turns into **✕** to close them, and **Esc** or clicking elsewhere closes them too.
- Switching pages now fades the new page in as it rises slightly. Pages outside the sidebar, such as destination search, **Custom CSS**, and the log, open as a circle growing from where you clicked, and close the same way. Switching between **Games** and **Players** no longer reloads the page.
- If a page fails to load, it now shows what went wrong with **Reload page**, **Go to Dashboard**, and **Open log folder**, instead of a blank window. The sidebar keeps working. If the whole window fails, it offers **Reload** and **Restart in safe mode**.
- Error messages now have a **Copy logs** button, and so do the screens shown when a page or the window fails to load. It copies the error, your Toolblox version and platform, and the last 200 log lines, ready to paste into a bug report. Cookies and other sign-in details are removed from the log.
- Loading screens, search results, online friends, logs, and widgets now show placeholder shapes while they load, instead of a spinning circle. Buttons show a spinning ring while they work, and placeholders shimmer.
- A short message in the corner now confirms when an action finishes: adding, removing, or signing in to an account, joining, pinning, saving a destination, and installing, updating, enabling, disabling, or uninstalling a widget. Turn on **Reduce toasts** in **Settings** to hide these. Errors still show, and an error replaces the newest success message instead of stacking on top of it.
- Hovering an account's destination or status badge, a dashboard notice, a widget's **Failed to load** label, or **Open folder** on **Logs** now shows the full text in a tooltip, instead of the slower system hint.
- Confirmation boxes, such as the one before deleting a group, are smaller, with their buttons beside the message, and only as tall as their text.
- **Settings** has a new **About** tab with the changelog, logs, legal links, and the app folder button. **Layout** in **General** is now **Interface**, and **Window** is now **Startup and window**.
- The Discord button moved from the top bar to a **Discord** row with a colored icon at the bottom of the sidebar.
- Widget icons in the sidebar are cropped to their artwork, so they show larger. The **Widgets** page is unchanged.
- **New group** at the top of **Accounts** is now an icon button, like the buttons beside it.
- The version number moved from the sidebar to a faint label in the bottom-right corner of the window.
- More settings, such as **Run in background**, **Multiple instances**, and **Auto-rejoin**, now have a short description of what they do.

## Animations
- Pages now build in section by section, with panels rising one after another, and the page title slides in.
- The sidebar highlight slides to the page you pick, and its icon bounces. Icons grow when you hover them.
- Switching between light and dark theme reveals the new theme as a circle from where you clicked.
- Buttons, switches, checkboxes, and toggles spring when pressed. Checkmarks spin in, and badges pop in.
- Menu items slide in one after another. Confirmation boxes spring up from below.
- Widget cards lift when you hover them, and their banner and icon zoom.
- An account's green **In game** dot pulses.
- Empty pages show a gently floating icon.
- **Show animations** in **Settings** turns all of these off. They also follow the system's reduced motion setting unless **Ignore system reduced motion** is on.
