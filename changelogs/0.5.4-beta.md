# Toolblox 0.5.4 beta
*Unreleased*

## New

### Widgets
- Widget icons can be animated GIFs. They play on **Widgets** and in the sidebar, and stay still when animations are off or reduced.
- Clicking a disabled widget in the sidebar asks to enable it, then opens it.
- Double-clicking an enabled widget in the sidebar disables it.

### Bootstrapper widget
- **Graphics level** and **Volume:** each profile now also sets Roblox's own **Graphics quality** and **Volume**. **Background** and **AFK** use the lowest graphics and no sound, and **Watch** keeps your own settings. They apply to every client opened while the Bootstrapper is on, including the one you're playing in, and your own settings come back when it's turned off. Change them under **Every client** on the **Bootstrapper** widget page. Clients already open keep theirs. **Custom** profiles keep your own settings until you change them.
- **Choose accounts:** while the Bootstrapper is on, each account in game has a **Bootstrapper** checkbox on **Accounts**. Uncheck it to return that client to normal right away. The choice is remembered, so the account stays unchecked when it rejoins. Check it to limit a client that was already open.
- **Open clients** on the **Bootstrapper** widget page lists every client with the same checkbox, including clients that aren't in game yet.

---

## Changes

### Confirmations
- Confirmation popups are slightly narrower, with a line under the title and the buttons beside the description.

### Autoclicker widget
- The widget has a new animated icon.

### Bootstrapper widget
- Limits now apply only to clients opened while **Join with Bootstrapper** is on. Clients already open when it's turned on, or when Toolblox starts, run normally.
- Turning the Bootstrapper off still returns every client to normal. Turning it back on limits the same checked clients again.

### Widget mark
- On account rows, the puzzle dot now sits after what the widget adds instead of covering it.

---

## Fixes

### Bootstrapper widget
- Clients are limited only once their account is in game, and only for accounts in Toolblox, so loading runs at full speed.
- Limits are undone when Toolblox closes or restarts, or the widget is updated or turned off. Before, clients could stay limited. Limits left by an earlier run are removed.
- A client opened by a limited client, such as on a rejoin, no longer starts limited.
- Your own frame rate, graphics, and volume are restored even when clients opened with the Bootstrapper close after it's turned off.
