Opens an isolated, incognito Roblox sign-in window.

After each page load the window’s cookies are polled for the session cookie. When found, the session is validated, the window’s browsing data is cleared, the session is stored, and notify receives the outcome. Navigation is limited to HTTPS Roblox hosts, and new windows are blocked.
