# Toolblox 0.4.7 beta
*Unreleased*

## New

### Join delay
- Set **Join delay** in **Settings → Accounts → Joining** to wait 0–60 seconds between accounts when joining several at once. The default is 1 second; failed requests can increase the wait.

### Online friends
- The dashboard lists the online friends of the **Continue as** account, as a row of avatars below **Pinned**. Hover a friend to see the game they're in. The list updates every minute.

### Widgets
- Widgets can share values with each other, so one can react to what another sees. See the host API reference.
- Anything that appears only because two widgets are installed together carries a link badge with the other widget's name. Hover it to see why it's there.

## New

### Rogue Lineage widget
- A new catalogue widget for Rogue Lineage shows your mana as a percentage over the mana bar. Install it from **Widgets**. It works on Windows.
- The widget's page has a tab for each feature, starting with **Mana bar overlay**. Turn the overlay on with the round power button on that tab. It finds the mana bar on its own while Roblox is focused, and reads your mana color the first time you charge.
- **Tracked spells:** **Add spell** lists **Gate** (75–85%), **Ignis** (45–55%), and **Snarvindur** (10–35%). Each tracked spell marks its range inside the bar, with its name beside it, and the trash button removes it. The range and the percentage light up while your mana is in range. A range close to your mana color is drawn in a lighter or darker shade.
- Select a tracked spell's color to change it. **Reset** returns a built-in spell to its own color.
- **Custom spell…** in the same menu adds a spell of your own with a name, a range, and a color.
- **Backfire warning:** turn on **Gate** to flash the screen red when Gate is cast outside 75–85%. It counts a cast only while Gate is equipped in the hotbar, from the click until the cast is confirmed. After the click it waits up to a second for the Gate prompt, then checks your mana several times. The tile shows **Gate equipped** while it is. **Test** shows the flash, and **Settings → Rogue Lineage → Warning sound** turns its sound on or off.
- **Cooldown overlay:** a second tab. Turn it on with its power button and it finds your hotbar on its own and reads which skill is in each slot. Press a skill's key, then click, and its slot is shaded until the cooldown ends. The shade shrinks from the top, and the seconds left show in the middle. **Skewer** (5 seconds) is supported for now, and more skills will follow.
- A click counts once the hotbar shows the skill's slot selected, so pressing a key and clicking right away works, and passing over a skill with its key doesn't start its cooldown. Shades stay put while the hotbar animates or is briefly hidden.
- A skill that's cooling down can't be restarted by clicking again. Pressing the same key twice clears the selection, as in the game.
- **Backpack:** with the cooldown overlay on, the widget sees when your backpack is open and reads the skill in each of its slots. A skill that's cooling down is shaded in the backpack too, with the seconds left.
- The tab shows each hotbar slot with its key and the name read from it, and lists the supported skills with their cooldowns. Names are read with the text recognition built into Windows.
- **Settings → Rogue Lineage → Cooldown overlay** sets the shade color, how see-through it is, and whether the countdown shows.
- **Debug mode:** turn it on in **Settings → Rogue Lineage → General**. Both overlays then label what they detect on screen, each with a line to where it was found: the mana bar with its percentage, color, and fill rate, the Gate text prompt, whether Gate is equipped, each hotbar and backpack slot with the name read from it, and a Gate cast as it's checked. Every step, and why one failed, goes to the Toolblox log, including when the backpack opens and closes.
- Both overlays work in a window or full screen, at any window size. In a small window, the hotbar's names can be too small to read, and the **Cooldown overlay** tab says so.
- The overlays read the screen on this device and never send input. The cooldown overlay watches the hotbar keys and left clicks only while it's on. Neither overlay appears in screenshots or recordings.

## Changes

### Dashboard
- **Pinned** accounts replace the **Accounts**, **In game**, and **Widgets enabled** tiles, directly below **Continue as**. Issue cards moved to the bottom of the page.

### Settings
- **Open app folder**, the folder button at the top of **Settings → General**, shows the Toolblox app folder in Explorer or Finder. Development builds also load unpacked widgets from its `widgets` folder.

### Log page
- The menu at the top left of the log shows **All sources**, only **Toolblox**, or one widget's lines. **Copy** copies what's shown.

### Autoclicker widget
- **Off** now turns every keybind off, so **Hold**, **Spam**, and other keys do nothing until the power button turns the autoclicker back on. Use it when switching to a game or account that shouldn't be autoclicked.
- **Paused** now means the autoclicker is on and keybinds work, but nothing is clicking right now. **Clicking** means it's clicking.
- The power button now turns the autoclicker on or off, and it stays that way after Toolblox restarts. Keybinds start and pause clicking.
- **Toggle** keybinds now work like the power button, turning the autoclicker on or off. They're the only keybinds that work while it's off.
- **Always on** is a new setting that keeps the autoclicker on, so every keybind always works. **Toggle** keys then start or pause clicking.
- The on-screen indicator shows the measured CPS while clicking, and **Paused** or **Off** otherwise.
- **Hold** keybinds now wait a moment before clicking, so a single click no longer sends extra clicks.
- **Pause** is a new keybind mode that starts or pauses clicking, like **Toggle** did before.
- **Spam** is a new keybind mode that clicks at the set speed while you spam its key. A single press, click, or hold doesn't start it, and clicking stops a moment after your last press. Bind it to left mouse to add clicks on top of your own. It works alongside every other mode.
- **Pausing for other widgets:** another installed widget can offer a reason to pause, shown below your keybinds as a switch, such as **Pause when backpack is open** from Rogue Lineage. Turn it on and clicking pauses while that applies, and the page shows **Paused by Rogue Lineage**. The backpack is then watched even with both Rogue Lineage overlays off.

## Fixes
- **Upgrading from the Python version:** Toolblox no longer fails to load with "A saved account contains session data". It removes the session data the old version saved with each account, and the account shows as signed out until it's added again.
