# Toolblox 0.4.0 beta
*October 22, 2026*

## Widgets
- **The Widgets tab is open.** Install, update, turn off, and remove widgets from the catalogue. Widgets are reviewed before they're listed.

## Performance
- **Tile windows:** arrange every open Roblox window in a grid, at full screen, medium, or small size. *(Windows)*
- **Client priority:** run alts at below normal or low priority, as a default or per account.
- **CPU cores per client:** spread clients across your CPU. *(Windows)*

## Dashboard
- **Now playing** and **Needs attention** (expired sessions, widget updates, no default place) replace Recently played. The dashboard fits the window without scrolling.
- **Theme**, **Snap to default aspect ratio**, and **Mini mode** buttons in the sidebar. Scroll them sideways to see the version.
- Select the Toolblox logo at the top of the sidebar to open the website.

## Mini mode
- A small bar that stays on top and drops down into your accounts, each with a join button. Messages show as system notifications while it's open.

## Accounts
- **Expired sessions** are detected and marked. Select **Sign in again** to fix one. Joining skips them and tells you which.

## Notifications
- Notifications show an icon, a title, and the details, and slide in from the corner away from the sidebar.
- They stack, show the time left, and close when selected.

## Also
- Slightly smaller corners throughout the app.
- An install guide at toolblox.dev/docs/install, covering the Windows and macOS security prompts.
- The Toolblox Discord bot, with `/link`, `/version`, and `/install`.
- toolblox.dev has an interactive preview with sample accounts, and a theme switch.
